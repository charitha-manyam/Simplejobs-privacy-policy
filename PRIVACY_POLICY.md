# PRIVACY POLICY

**Last updated: September 17, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how SimpleJobs ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when you use the SimpleJobs mobile application (the "App"). By downloading and using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account information:** When you register, we collect your mobile number (required), password, and optionally your email address.
- **Candidate profile information:** Full name, gender, date of birth, qualifications, work experience, current and expected salary, category, current and preferred location, availability, passport status, overseas experience, summary, skills, and languages.
- **Employer / company information:** Company name, contact person, industry, company about, and bulk-hiring inquiry details (company, contact person, email, phone, mobile number, employee count, roles, number of openings, and message).
- **Job application information:** Resume/CV documents (PDF, DOC, DOCX), cover letters, interview scheduling details, interview results and feedback, and job offers (salary, joining date, work location, benefits, and terms).
- **Staff/Admin information:** For staff and admin users, we collect name, mobile number, email, role, department, and password.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device information:** When you enable push notifications, we collect your device push notification token and platform (e.g., Android or iOS) to deliver notifications to you.
- **Usage information:** We may collect basic technical information necessary to operate and secure the App.

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Location (GPS):** We do not track your device location. Location details are only provided by you as free-text (e.g., current or preferred city).
- **Photo/Gallery/Camera:** We do not access your camera, photo library, or gallery.
- **Contacts:** We do not access your device contacts.
- **Payment information:** We do not collect or process payment card details through the App.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
- To create and manage your account and verify your identity.
- To allow you to build and update your candidate or employer profile.
- To allow you to browse, search, post, and apply for jobs.
- To match candidates with job opportunities and employers with candidates.
- To let employers send interview invitations and job offers.
- To send you push notifications about application status, interviews, and messages.
- To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
- To maintain the security, integrity, and operation of the App.
- To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

---

## 3. How We Store and Share Your Information

- **Storage:** Your personal information is stored on secure servers operated by us or our service providers. Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.
- **Push notifications:** We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (on Android) and Apple Push Notification Service (on iOS) to deliver push notifications. Your push token and device platform are shared with these providers solely for the purpose of delivering notifications.
- **Third parties:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes, except as required by law or to provide core App functionality (such as push notification delivery).
- **Service providers:** We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the App (e.g., hosting and push notification services), under obligations of confidentiality.

---

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, including:
- Encrypted storage of your authentication token on your device.
- Restricted access to personal data to authorized personnel only.
- Industry-standard security practices for our servers and networks.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:
- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correction:** Update or correct inaccurate information through your profile.
- **Deletion:** Request the deletion of your personal information, subject to legal requirements.
- **Opt-out of notifications:** Disable push notifications anytime through your device settings.

You may also close your account by contacting us, after which we will delete or anonymize your personal information, subject to legitimate business and legal retention requirements.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

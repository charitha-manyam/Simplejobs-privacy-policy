How to request deletion

To permanently delete your account and the data associated with it, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email from the address you registered with to kramya31793@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete account request.
  3. In the body, include the mobile number registered on your SimpleJobs account and confirm that you want your account permanently deleted.
  4. To verify the request, we may reply and ask you to confirm once more before we proceed.

We process valid deletion requests within 30 days of receiving them.

Tip: You can also request deletion by raising a support request from inside the app (Dashboard → Contact support) with the subject “Delete my account”.

What happens when you request deletion

When your deletion request is approved, your account is closed and you will no longer be able to sign in to SimpleJobs.

Processing stepTimeline
Account is deactivated and sign-in is disabled Immediately on approval
Your personal data and uploaded files are permanently deleted Within 30 days of approval
Records we are legally required to keep are erased at the end of the required period As required by applicable law (up to 3 years)

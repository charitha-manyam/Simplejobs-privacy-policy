You can request that your personal data be deleted at any time. Deleting your data does not require you to delete your account, and you can continue using the App afterwards.

What Happens When You Request Deletion

We will permanently delete the personal information and content associated with your account that we hold (such as your profile, job listings, applications, and other data you have provided), subject to legal retention requirements. Your account itself will remain active.

How to Submit a Request

  1. Send an email to kramya31793@gmail.com with the subject line: "Data Deletion Request - SimpleJobs".
  2. Use the email address or mobile number that you registered with in the App so we can verify your identity.
  3. Specify which data you would like deleted (for example: "all my data" or "only my job postings").

What Happens Next

  • We will verify your request, usually within 3 business days.
  • Once verified, your requested data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • We will send a confirmation email once your request has been processed.

Deleting Your Entire Account

If you want to delete your account and all data associated with it, you can also request this through the same email address. Deleting your account will permanently remove your profile and all data you have provided.

Need Help?

For any questions about your data or privacy, contact us at kramya31793@gmail.com.
